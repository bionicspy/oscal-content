# Public Communications
**Pattern URI:** `urn:utoronto:security:pattern:public-communications`

## Purpose

The **public-communications** pattern defines obligations related to **official institutional communications** delivered to external or broad audiences.

This pattern asserts that public-facing communications are subject to **branding, approval, accessibility, and reputational controls** governed by institutional authorities.

---

## Scope

This pattern applies to systems that:

- Disseminate messages to external audiences
- Represent the institution publicly
- Communicate beyond private or internal operational contexts

---

## Normative Assertions

A system asserting **public-communications** guarantees that:

1. Communications represent the institution in an **authorized and approved manner**.
2. Branding, messaging, and sender identity requirements are **enforced**.
3. Accessibility and disclosure obligations are **applied consistently**.
4. Unauthorized public communication is **prevented**.

---

## Out of Scope

This pattern does not define:

- Messaging content guidelines
- Visual design standards
- Approval workflows
- Campaign strategy

---

## Relationship to Other Patterns

This pattern is commonly composed with:

- `regulated-digital-service`
- `email-bulk-messaging-gateway`
- `secure-identity`

---

## Summary

The **public-communications** pattern establishes institutional safeguards around who may speak publicly and under what authority, without prescribing messaging details.
